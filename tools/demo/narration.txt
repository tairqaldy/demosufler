01. This is demosufler. A teleprompter that lives right under your Mac's camera, so you read your script while looking straight into the lens.
02. Set your own pace, and see how long the script will take. Fade the panel when you need to see what is behind it.
03. Record your camera, your screen, or both at once, each into its own file.
04. The prompter floats above every app, and it never shows up in your recording.
05. Press record. Three, two, one, and you are live.
06. Launch mode goes one step further. When you press stop, it builds a finished video for you. It zooms in on every click and frames your screen on a clean background. This video was made with it.
07. It is free and open source, it speaks English and Russian, and it updates itself in one click.
08. demosufler. Free for your Mac.
